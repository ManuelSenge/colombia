5–19 December 2026 · 14 nights

Colombia,
before the wedding.

Land in Bogotá on the evening of the 5th. Fly to Salento the next morning and walk for three days from Valle de Cocora to Paramillo del Quindío. Land in Medellín on the afternoon of the 9th, fit the suit that evening and keep the 10th for the wedding. Coffee with friends near Medellín, then Cartagena, Taganga and Tayrona. Home from Bogotá on the 19th.

In Bogotá, 5 Dec evening Trek 6–8 Dec Medellín afternoon of the 9th Wedding 10 Dec Out Bogotá, 19 Dec

The 9th is an afternoon landing either way. Avianca has one direct Armenia (AXM) → Medellín (MDE), around 15:20, and both versions are back in Salento on the evening of the 8th. The 3-day version starts walking late on Sunday the 6th and summits. The 2-day version uses the 6th for Salento and the briefing, then walks the páramo on the 7th and 8th.

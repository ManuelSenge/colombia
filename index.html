5–19 December 2026 · 14 nights

Colombia,
before the wedding.

Land in Bogotá at 19:35 on the 5th and sleep by the airport. Fly to Armenia at 05:10, spend the day in Salento, then walk three days to the summit of Paramillo del Quindío. A taxi to Pereira that evening and an early flight to Medellín on the 10th for the suit and the pre-wedding dinner. The wedding is on the 11th. Coffee with friends, Comuna 13, then Cartagena, Taganga and Tayrona. Home from Bogotá on the 19th.

  • Páramo Trek summit, 7–9 Dec. Their briefing is at the Salento office before 16:00 on the 6th.
  • The 05:10 Bogotá → Armenia and the 07:10 Pereira → Medellín flights.
  • Casa París for the morning of the 10th.
  • A taxi from Salento to Pereira on the evening of the 9th — the trek ends around 18:00 and the last bus leaves about then.

Colombia,
before the wedding.

Land in Bogotá on the evening of the 5th. Fly to Salento the next morning and walk for three days from Valle de Cocora to Paramillo del Quindío. Land in Medellín on the afternoon of the 9th, keep the 11th for the wedding, then Cartagena, Taganga and Tayrona. Home from Bogotá on the 19th.

In Bogotá, 5 Dec evening Trek 6–8 Dec Medellín afternoon of the 9th Wedding 11 Dec Out Bogotá, 19 Dec

The 9th is an afternoon landing. Avianca has one direct Armenia (AXM) → Medellín (MDE), around 15:20. The trek ends in Salento on the 8th, so that flight is the plan, not a backup. Tell the guide you can start late on the morning of the 6th, after the Bogotá flight.
